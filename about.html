Our Story

Your Majesty Event Vendors was built to bring premium quality and reliable execution to event photo booths in Toronto, Barrie, and the GTA. We noticed many hosts had to choose between affordable options and professional quality, so we designed a service that delivers both.

From venue walkthrough to event day setup, we focus on smooth logistics, clear communication, and a guest experience that feels effortless. Our team supports weddings, brand activations, and private events with a setup that blends into your event design while creating standout photo moments.
