Barrie events have a different rhythm than downtown Toronto events. Barrie Photo Booth Rental Questions

How much space does the booth need?

Plan for roughly 8 by 8 feet with a standard electrical outlet nearby. Ask your venue about access, placement, and setup time; tell us about any restrictions before booking.

Are travel fees included?

Travel fees may apply depending on your venue. Send the address with your event details so any travel charge can be confirmed in your quote.
