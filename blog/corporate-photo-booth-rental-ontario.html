Corporate Photo Booth Rentals in Ontario: Make Your Next Event Unforgettable

May 6, 2025 • 5 min read

A photo booth at a corporate event does more than entertain guests — it puts your brand in front of everyone who attends, and sends it home with them. Here's why more Ontario businesses are adding photo booths to their event lineup.

Why photo booths work so well at corporate events

Corporate events can sometimes feel stiff — especially when guests don't know each other well. A photo booth breaks the ice immediately. It gives attendees something fun and interactive to do, creates natural conversation starters, and produces branded content that travels far beyond the event itself.

Every photo or video that leaves the booth has your logo, event name, or campaign branding embedded directly into it. When guests share that content on LinkedIn, Instagram, or internally within their organization, your brand goes with it. It's organic promotion that feels personal rather than promotional — and that's a combination most marketing budgets can't easily buy.

The best photo booth setups for corporate events in Ontario

The 360 video booth is the most popular choice for corporate events right now. It produces a premium, eye-catching slow-motion video clip with your logo overlaid — exactly the kind of content that performs well on social media and gets shared. It also draws a crowd, which keeps energy levels high throughout the event.

The mirror booth is a strong choice for more formal corporate settings like galas, awards dinners, and client appreciation events. It's elegant, unobtrusive, and produces branded physical prints that attendees take home — a tangible reminder of your event and your company.

For trade shows, product launches, and larger conferences, an open-air booth with a branded backdrop can accommodate large groups quickly and keeps throughput high when you have hundreds of attendees.

How branding works

Every corporate booking includes a fully custom-designed digital overlay featuring your company logo, event name, date, campaign hashtag, or any other branding element you need. We work with your marketing team or event coordinator ahead of time to get the design right — most clients receive a proof within 48 hours of booking. We make revisions until it's exactly what you need.

Custom branded backdrops are also available. A branded step-and-repeat backdrop (the kind you see at press events and product launches) turns every photo into a professional branded asset. Combined with the overlay, every capture becomes marketing content.

Types of corporate events we serve in Ontario

Your Majesty Event Vendors provides corporate photo booth rentals for company holiday parties, team building events, product launches, trade shows and expos, client appreciation events, awards galas, conferences and summits, and grand openings. What to tell your event coordinator

When you're planning a corporate event and considering a photo booth, a few things are worth confirming early: the floor space available at your venue, whether you need physical prints or just digital delivery, how many guests you expect and over what time period, and whether branding assets like your logo are available in high resolution. We'll take care of the rest.
