Photo Booth Rental in Barrie, Ontario (2026 Guide)

April 1, 2025 • 6 min read · By YM Event Vendors

Planning an event in Barrie and want to give your guests something to remember? A photo booth is one of the easiest ways to make any event more fun — and we're breaking down exactly what to look for, what's available, and how to book the right setup for your event.

Why rent a photo booth for your Barrie event?

Photo booths have become one of the most popular event add-ons in Ontario — and for good reason. They give guests something interactive to do, create shareable moments for social media, and leave everyone with a personalized keepsake. Unlike hiring additional photographers, a photo booth works all night without a break. Guests can return to it multiple times, try different props, and share digital copies instantly. It's entertainment, a marketing tool, and a party favour all rolled into one.

Photo booth styles available in Barrie

Not all photo booths are the same. Here's a breakdown of the most popular options:

360 video booth

The 360 booth is the most in-demand option right now. Guests stand on a platform while a camera arm rotates around them, capturing slow-motion video from every angle. The result is a shareable clip that looks incredible on Instagram and TikTok. Perfect for weddings, corporate events, and milestone birthdays.

Mirror photo booth

The mirror booth is a full-length interactive mirror that photographs guests, adds fun animations and on-screen text, and prints a physical copy on the spot. It's elegant, easy to use, and fits beautifully into any décor — from upscale weddings to school grad events.

Open-air photo booth

A classic for a reason. The open-air setup fits more people in the frame, works great with custom backdrops, and keeps the energy open and social. Great for birthday parties, grad events, and community gatherings.
