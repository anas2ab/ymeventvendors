Photo Booth Rental in Barrie, Ontario: Packages & Pricing

April 1, 2025 • Updated September 27, 2026 · By Your Majesty Event Vendors

Planning an event in Barrie? Compare our two priced photo booth packages, what each includes, and the cost of adding an extra hour.

Photo booth packages and prices in Barrie

Our two priced packages start with a two-hour rental. Both include digital photos and sharing, a choice of backdrop, unlimited 2×6 prints, and an optional prop table.

Signature Prints — $400 CAD

Signature Prints is $400 CAD for two hours. Additional time is $100 per hour. Unlimited 4×6 prints are available as a $25-per-hour add-on.

Glam Booth — $500 CAD

Glam Booth is $500 CAD for two hours and includes unlimited 4×6 prints and premium envelopes for guests. Additional time is $150 per hour.

Serving Barrie and Simcoe County

We provide photo booth rentals in Barrie and Simcoe County for weddings, corporate events and celebrations. When you request a quote, include your venue or event location, date, preferred package and rental duration so we can confirm availability and any travel details.

How much does a photo booth rental cost in Barrie?

Both packages include unlimited 2×6 prints. Glam Booth also includes unlimited 4×6 prints and premium envelopes. Signature Prints can add unlimited 4×6 prints for $25 per hour.
