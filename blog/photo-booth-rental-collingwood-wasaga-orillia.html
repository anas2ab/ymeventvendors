Photo Booth Rental in Collingwood, Wasaga Beach & Orillia

May 13, 2025 • 4 min read

Your Majesty Event Vendors brings premium photo booth experiences to Collingwood, Wasaga Beach, Orillia, and every community in between. Here's what's available and how to book.

We serve the full Barrie region

Based in Barrie, we regularly travel to events across Simcoe County and the surrounding area. Whether your event is at a ski chalet in Collingwood, a beachfront venue in Wasaga Beach, a banquet hall in Orillia, or anywhere in between — we'll be there on time, set up, and ready to go.

Communities we serve include Collingwood, Wasaga Beach, Orillia, Innisfil, Angus, New Tecumseth, Midland, Penetanguishene, Bradford, and Alliston. If your community isn't listed, reach out — we cover a wide region and are happy to confirm whether your venue is within our service area.

Photo booth rental in Collingwood

Collingwood is one of Ontario's most popular event destinations — with ski resorts, golf clubs, and wedding venues that attract guests from across the province. We've set up photo booths at Collingwood events ranging from intimate winter weddings to large corporate retreats. Our setups travel and install easily in any venue, and we handle all logistics so your event runs smoothly.

Popular Collingwood events we service: weddings at ski resort venues, corporate team retreats, holiday parties, graduation celebrations, and birthday milestones.

Photo booth rental in Wasaga Beach

Wasaga Beach is a summer events hub — and photo booths are a perfect match for the relaxed, celebratory energy of events here. Beach-themed props, open-air setups, and high-energy 360 booths all work beautifully at Wasaga Beach venues. We service events all summer long in the area and can accommodate both indoor and outdoor-adjacent setups.

Popular Wasaga Beach events we service: summer birthday parties, family reunions, engagement parties, community events, and beach-adjacent wedding receptions.

Photo booth rental in Orillia

Orillia hosts everything from intimate family celebrations to large fundraising galas and corporate events. Our full range of photo booth options — 360 booth, mirror booth, and open-air — are all available for Orillia events. Travel time from Barrie is minimal, so there are no significant travel fees for most Orillia bookings.

Popular Orillia events we service: wedding receptions, charity galas, school events, corporate parties, and anniversary celebrations.

What's included when we travel to your event

All out-of-Barrie bookings include the same full package: professional delivery and setup, a friendly on-site attendant for the full rental period, unlimited digital captures, a custom branded digital overlay, and full gallery delivery after your event. Travel fees may apply for venues more than 30 minutes from Barrie — we'll always disclose this clearly in your quote.

How to book

Ready to book a photo booth for your Collingwood, Wasaga Beach, or Orillia event? Fill out our inquiry form with your event date, venue location, and the type of booth you're interested in. We'll confirm availability and send you a quote within 24 hours. We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance for summer and holiday events.
