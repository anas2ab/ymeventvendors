Planning a wedding, party or corporate event in Collingwood, Wasaga Beach or Orillia? Use this Simcoe County guide to compare our photo booth packages and prepare the venue details needed for a quote.

Photo booth packages for Simcoe County events

Your Majesty Event Vendors serves Barrie and Simcoe County, including Collingwood, Wasaga Beach and Orillia. Share your exact venue address and event date so we can confirm availability, travel requirements and setup access.

Our Signature Prints and Glam Booth packages start at $400 CAD and $500 CAD respectively for two hours. Both include digital photos and sharing, a choice of backdrop, unlimited 2×6 prints and an optional prop table. Glam Booth also includes unlimited 4×6 prints and premium envelopes. See the package pricing guide for extra-hour and print-upgrade rates.

Planning a photo booth rental in Collingwood

For a resort, golf-club or country-venue event, ask your coordinator where vendors can unload and how the booth reaches the reception room. Share any stairs, elevator reservations and setup restrictions in your inquiry. If guests move between cocktail and reception spaces, choose a booth location that stays accessible during the paid rental window.

Planning a photo booth rental in Wasaga Beach

For a beach-area celebration or a venue with outdoor space, confirm an indoor or sheltered setup with reliable power. Discuss weather protection and a backup location before booking; a nearby beach does not mean equipment can be set up directly on sand. Leave a clear route between the booth, seating and refreshments.

Planning a photo booth rental in Orillia

For a wedding reception, community celebration or company party, share the guest count and the time guests can use the booth. A nearby outlet, room for the backdrop and a separate place to collect prints help avoid a queue across doorways or service routes. Confirm load-in, parking and travel costs for your specific venue.

What to include in your regional event quote

  • Event date, venue name and full address.
  • Guest count, event type and desired booth hours.
  • Preferred package, print format and customization requests.
  • Available floor space, power, loading access and setup window.

Travel charges depend on the event location and arrangements; confirm them in the quote. Also confirm attendant support, gallery delivery and booking terms for your selected package. For reception planning, read our Barrie and Simcoe County wedding photo booth guide.
