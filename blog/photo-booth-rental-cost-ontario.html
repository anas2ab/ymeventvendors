How Much Does a Photo Booth Rental Cost in Ontario?

April 15, 2025 • Updated September 27, 2026 · By Your Majesty Event Vendors

Our two priced photo booth packages start at $400 CAD for Signature Prints and $500 CAD for Glam Booth, each for two hours. Here’s what’s included and how extra-hour pricing works for events in Toronto, Barrie and the GTA.

Our Ontario photo booth package prices

Both packages start with a two-hour rental. Choose the one that fits your event and print preference:

Signature Prints — $400 CAD

The two-hour Signature Prints package includes digital photos and sharing, a choice of backdrop, and unlimited 2×6 prints. An optional prop table is available. Extra time is $100 per hour. Unlimited 4×6 prints can be added for $25 per hour.

Glam Booth — $500 CAD

The two-hour Glam Booth package includes digital photos and sharing, a choice of backdrop, unlimited 2×6 and 4×6 prints, and premium envelopes for guests. An optional prop table is available. Extra time is $150 per hour.

What to include in your quote request

When you request a quote, share your event date, venue or city, preferred package, and the number of hours you need. That lets us confirm availability and any event-specific details, including travel or requested add-ons.
