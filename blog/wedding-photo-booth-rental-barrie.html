Wedding Photo Booth Rental in Barrie, Ontario: Everything You Need to Know

April 22, 2025 • Updated September 29, 2026 · By Your Majesty Event Vendors

Planning a wedding in Barrie or Simcoe County? Start with the print format, rental hours and space available at your reception. This guide explains our current packages and the details to confirm with your venue before booking.

Which photo booth package fits your wedding?

Our photo booth packages both include digital photos and sharing, a choice of backdrop, unlimited 2×6 prints, and an optional prop table. Choose your package based on the keepsakes you want guests to take home:

  • Signature Prints: $400 CAD for two hours, with additional time at $100 per hour. Unlimited 4×6 prints are a $25-per-hour add-on.
  • Glam Booth: $500 CAD for two hours, including unlimited 2×6 and 4×6 prints and premium envelopes. Additional time is $150 per hour.

These are starting package prices. Include your venue address and any custom requests when asking for a quote so that travel and other event-specific costs can be confirmed.

When should the booth be open during your reception?

Compare your rental window with the time guests will be free to use the booth. Cocktail hour can work if the booth is near the reception space. After dinner is another option, particularly when speeches and the first dance would interrupt an earlier session.

A two-hour package may suit a focused reception window; a longer guest list or a break between cocktail hour and dancing may call for extra time. Confirm when paid booth hours begin, whether there is idle time during dinner, and the cost of extending the session.

What should you confirm with your Barrie venue?

Our usual planning guideline is about an 8×8 ft setup area with a nearby standard electrical outlet. Confirm the actual footprint for your selected setup, and leave additional room for a queue, props and print collection. Keep the booth clear of emergency exits, serving routes and the dance floor.

Ask your coordinator about loading access, stairs or elevators, parking and the permitted setup window. For rural or outdoor venues in Simcoe County, confirm a suitable indoor or sheltered location, reliable power and a weather backup before finalizing the layout. Our Barrie wedding venue layout guide covers these planning checks in more detail.

How can you personalize the photos?

Print templates can feature your names, wedding date, monogram or wedding colours. Share your design ideas when requesting a quote and confirm the available backdrop options, any custom-design charges and when your template must be approved.

The prop table is optional. A simple backdrop and a few coordinated props can work well if you want the photographs to match your reception décor. See our wedding photo booth backdrop and prop ideas for inspiration.

What should you send when checking availability?

Send your wedding date, venue name and address, guest count, preferred package and proposed booth hours. Ask for written confirmation of travel charges, attendant support, gallery delivery, payment terms and cancellation terms. Check availability once your date and venue are set, including for a last-minute event.
