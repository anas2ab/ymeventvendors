Privacy Policy

Learn how Your Majesty Event Vendors collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective February 22, 2026 Customer Data and Inquiries

1. Information we collect

When you submit a form, request a quote, subscribe to updates, or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, event details, and any information you choose to provide.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide quotes, schedule services, communicate about bookings, and improve our website and service experience.

3. Marketing communications

If you subscribe to updates, we may send occasional emails related to offers, event tips, or services. You can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted third-party tools (for example, form or email service providers) to process communications necessary to operate our business.

5. Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed for business, legal, or customer service purposes.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Your choices

You can request updates or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at contact@ymeventvendors.ca.
