Services FAQ

Quick answers for package selection and booking decisions.

Which package is best for weddings?

Most wedding clients choose Signature Prints for keepsake value or Glam Booth when they want the most elevated package.

Can I start with Digital and upgrade later?

Yes. If timeline and equipment availability allow it, you can upgrade before your event date.

Do you offer branded templates for corporate events?

Yes. We can apply logos, brand colors, and campaign treatments to templates and overlays.

How much space should we reserve at the venue?

Plan for roughly 8x8 ft with power access. We can advise on layout if your venue has space limits.
